5 · Metric Cards (Metric → Value → Meaning → Interpretation)

No unexplained numbers — every metric carries its meaning, per PRD §19.

Debt Service Coverage Ratio
1.45×
What it measures: whether assessed cash flow covers debt-service obligations.
Interpretation: assessed cash flow is 1.45× the debt-service requirement — above the 1.20× policy minimum. Adequate cover.
Loan-to-Value (collateral)
68%
What it measures: proposed exposure relative to verified security value.
Interpretation: below the 75% policy cap for SBL. Collateral supports — but does not override — repayment capacity.

6 · Risk Rows (Risk → Why it matters → Mitigation)

Warnings always explain themselves, per PRD §9.

⚠ High existing debt exposure HIGH
Why it matters: existing obligations consume 41% of verified cash flow, leaving thin buffer for the new facility.
✓ Mitigation: supportable amount haircut applied (₦5.0m → ₦3.2m); condition: settle Facility B before disbursement.
⚠ Revenue concentration — 1 buyer ≈ 55% of sales MEDIUM
Why it matters: loss of the anchor buyer would cut cash flow below the DSCR floor within one quarter.
✓ Mitigation: 24-month supply contract verified; covenant: report buyer-share quarterly.
